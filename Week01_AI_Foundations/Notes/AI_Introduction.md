# Artificial Intelligence — Introduction

## What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science that focuses on creating machines and software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples include:

- Understanding language
- Recognizing images
- Making predictions
- Solving problems
- Learning from data
- Making decisions

## Examples of AI

- ChatGPT
- Voice assistants
- Recommendation systems
- Face recognition
- Self-driving technology
- Fraud detection
- Medical image analysis

## How AI Works

A simple AI workflow is:

Data
↓
Processing
↓
Learning / Reasoning
↓
Prediction or Decision
↓
Output

## Traditional Programming vs AI

Traditional programming:

Input + Rules → Output

Machine Learning:

Data + Algorithm → Model

New Data + Trained Model → Prediction

## Types of AI

### Narrow AI

AI designed to perform a specific task.

Examples:

- Recommendation systems
- Spam detection
- Face recognition

### General AI

A theoretical form of AI capable of performing a wide range of intellectual tasks similar to humans.

### Superintelligent AI

A theoretical concept referring to AI that would exceed human intelligence across many areas.

## Key AI Areas

- Machine Learning
- Deep Learning
- Natural Language Processing
- Computer Vision
- Robotics
- Generative AI

## Conclusion

AI allows computers to perform tasks involving learning, reasoning, pattern recognition, prediction, and decision-making.

# AI Applications

AI is used across many industries.

## Healthcare

Applications:

- Medical image analysis
- Disease prediction
- Drug discovery
- Patient monitoring

## Finance

Applications:

- Fraud detection
- Credit risk analysis
- Algorithmic trading
- Customer support

## Education

Applications:

- Personalized learning
- AI tutors
- Automated assessment
- Learning recommendations

## Retail

Applications:

- Product recommendations
- Customer analysis
- Demand forecasting
- Inventory optimization

## Transportation

Applications:

- Route optimization
- Autonomous driving technology
- Traffic prediction
- Fleet management

## Manufacturing

Applications:

- Predictive maintenance
- Quality inspection
- Robotics
- Production optimization

## Natural Language Processing

NLP allows computers to work with human language.

Examples:

- Chatbots
- Translation
- Sentiment analysis
- Text summarization

## Computer Vision

Computer Vision allows computers to analyze images and videos.

Examples:

- Face recognition
- Object detection
- Medical image analysis
- Quality inspection

## Generative AI

Generative AI can create:

- Text
- Images
- Audio
- Video
- Code
